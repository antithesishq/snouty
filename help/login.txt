Sign in and store your snouty configuration

Sign in to your tenant, then save the tenant in the global snouty settings file and store your credential, optionally under a named profile. Login checks the credential with the tenant, and saves nothing when the tenant rejects it. Information not provided via args is asked for at the terminal. Without a terminal, login checks the credential that other commands use, such as ANTITHESIS_API_KEY, and stores no credential.

Use {{ t("--registry")? }} to push your images to a registry other than your tenant's, such as `registry.example.com/team`: a host, optionally with a path. Then sign in to that registry with `docker login` or `podman login`.

NOTE: `{{ t("snouty login")? }}` will offer to reuse your existing configuration values, including any sourced from a local .snouty.toml file or the file specified by {{ t("--settings")? }} or via the SNOUTY_SETTINGS_PATH environment variable. However, snouty login will save the specified configuration and credentials to the "global" files in your home directory.

Examples:
  {{ t("snouty login")? }}
  {{ t("snouty login --tenant \"mytenant\" --registry \"registry.example.com/team\"")? }}
  {{ t("snouty login --profile \"profile\"")? }}
